Cinder Quay is an open-world crime game set in a sun-soaked harbour city at dawn. You are a dawn-shift courier who gets pulled into the harbour underworld: walk the streets or steal a car and drive, run five story missions across the container yard, rail yard, market and pier, take procedural dispatch jobs, claim turf from a rival crew, and outrun the harbour patrol as your wanted level climbs from one star to five. Traffic follows the roads, pedestrians flee danger, police chase and ram, and turf you own pays rent while rivals try to take it back. Everything is drawn on a 2D canvas with a warm dawn palette, a live minimap, a clock that runs from 05:30 to morning, and sound synthesised live with WebAudio. No assets are borrowed from any commercial game: the city, characters, story and music are all original.

## How to run

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network request while playing.

## Controls

WASD or arrow keys to walk and drive, Shift to run, E to enter or leave a car, Space for the handbrake, F to punch, J for a dispatch job, Enter to start the next mission, Esc or P to pause, M to mute.
